Get supported on the platform you already use

You don't move. Your videos stay on your PeerTube channel, your music stays on your server, your stream stays yours. Supporters who opt in send you a little for every view, listen, or minute — automatically.

  • Tips, not tollbooths Your content stays open by default. Supporters who've set it up pay as they enjoy it; everyone else keeps watching for free. Locking things behind payment is possible, but it's a switch — not the point.
  • You keep 100% There's no platform cut and no payment-processor fee inside the rail. A supporter's tip arrives whole.
  • Nobody holds the money Supporters keep their funds in their own vault; payments settle directly to your wallet. There's no company account in the middle that can freeze, delay, or fold.
  • Any size instance Works the same on a huge instance or a self-hosted box with three viewers.

How it feels in practice

A supporter sets aside some money once and decides a budget — say, "up to $5 this month." Then they just watch and listen like they always do. Every counted view or finished song sends a tiny amount (you and your admin choose the rate — fractions of a cent per event are normal) straight to your wallet. They can stop at any time, and they can never be charged past the budget they set.

What you need

A wallet address, and an instance whose admin has attached the rail (or a platform where you can run the attachment yourself — it never modifies the platform). Send your admin to the admin overview; the setup is a sidecar or a plugin install, not a migration.

Find your platform

  • PeerTube

    Federated VOD published plugin tested

    Install the published universal-paywall plugin — per-view support/tips settled on-chain, no PeerTube fork.

  • Owncast

    Live video event sidecar tested

    Register an admin webhook for chat presence events — viewers support the streamer per second watched.

  • Navidrome

    Music config redirect tested

    Point ND_LISTENBRAINZ_BASEURL at the sidecar — every play pays the artist, per listen.

  • Subsonic (gonic family)

    Music config redirect tested

    Meter the Subsonic scrobble endpoint — per-play royalties for gonic and other Subsonic-API servers.

  • Jellyfin

    VOD event sidecar tested

    Point the official Webhook plugin at the sidecar — watched minutes settle to the library owner.

  • RSSHub

    Feeds event sidecar tested

    A per-citation toll at the crawler boundary — AI agents pay authors when they ground answers in feed items.

  • Immich

    Photo reverse proxy tested

    A transparent reverse proxy in front of Immich meters shared-link photo resolves — a per-resolve license fee.

  • Mastodon

    Fediverse external provider tested

    Serve Mastodon's donation-campaign slot — instance donations settle on-chain, transparently.

  • MusicBrainz

    Registry / resolver resolver tested

    The identity registry that turns a music scrobble into a payable artist — recording MBID → artist MBID → wallet.

Your audience pays through the browser extension or any client that speaks the rail — that side is covered too.
